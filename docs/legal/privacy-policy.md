# Privacy Policy

**Effective July 2026 — docs.projectjupiter.in**

This policy describes data practices for the **BRIEFR documentation portal**
only (`https://docs.projectjupiter.in`). It does **not** govern:

- The **live demo** at `https://briefrdemo.projectjupiter.in` — a static
  fixture preview with no accounts or backend.
- Any **self-hosted** BRIEFR deployment — your operator is the data fiduciary.
- The **Project Jupiter marketing site** at `https://projectjupiter.in` — see
  [its privacy policy](https://projectjupiter.in/privacy).

**Data Fiduciary (this docs site):** Sai Harsha Vardhan.

## 1. What we do not collect on this docs site

- No first-party analytics (no Google Analytics, Plausible, or similar scripts
  embedded in this documentation build).
- No accounts, sign-in, or comment forms on this portal.
- No intentional collection of your name, email, or stack profile through this
  static site.
- No sale or sharing of visitor data with advertisers.

## 2. What may be processed automatically

### Hosting (Cloudflare)

This site is served through **Cloudflare** (CDN / Workers). Cloudflare may
process connection metadata (for example IP address, request path, User-Agent,
and timestamps) for delivery, security, and abuse protection under
[Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
We do not receive a separate marketing database of visitors from Cloudflare.

### Local search

Search uses **`@easyops-cn/docusaurus-search-local`**: the index is built at
compile time and queried in your browser. Search queries are not sent to our
servers or to Algolia.

### Fonts

Typography is **self-hosted** via `@fontsource` packages. Your browser does not
load fonts from Google Fonts or other third-party CDNs when reading this site.

### Outbound links

Pages link to **GitHub** (source, edit URLs) and to external references in
learning chapters. Following those links is subject to each destination's
policies.

## 3. Cookies

This documentation build does not set first-party authentication or preference
cookies. Cloudflare or your browser may set strictly necessary technical cookies
for delivery and security; we do not use them for cross-site tracking on this
portal.

## 4. Children's privacy

This technical documentation is intended for security practitioners and
builders. It is not directed at children under 13.

## 5. Your rights

Because we do not maintain user accounts on this portal, there is typically no
personal profile to export or erase here. If you believe connection logs held
by our hosting provider relate to you and wish to raise a request, contact our
Grievance Officer (below). For data held by a **self-hosted BRIEFR instance**
or the static live demo, contact that operator or see the in-app privacy page
on that deployment.

## 6. Changes

We may update this policy when hosting or site behavior changes. The effective
date at the top will be revised accordingly.
